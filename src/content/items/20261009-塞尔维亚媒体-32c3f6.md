# Novi Sad Gymnasium to Return to Main Building by October 15th

Students and teachers of Novi Sad's Gymnasium 'Jovan Jovanović Zmaj' will return to their main building before October 15th, announced the mayor of Novi Sad, Žarko Mićin. He mentioned that after the completion of work on the dome and the removal of parts that did not comply with building permits, the City Administration for Inspection Services, specifically the Sector of Construction Inspection, decided to lift the ban on using the ceremonial hall. With the completion of all installations, regular classes will resume next week, Mićin said on Instagram. The safety of the object has been verified, and after the work was completed, judicial expertise, an inspection of the object, and a test load test were carried out. It was confirmed that the main dome and the work done on its destruction and height correction do not threaten the safety of the entire object. Mićin highlighted that the City, the Province, and the Republic, in collaboration with the gymnasium management, have worked together to ensure that students return to their classrooms after two years. The object will be monitored for the next six months, the mayor announced, adding that the safety of students and staff is the absolute priority, as reported by Tanjug.
